You didn't miss the deadline. You just never saw it.

Muhurat is a launch clock for student opportunities: real contests, hackathons
and scholarships pulled live from public sources, ranked by the time you
actually have — and turned into reminders that reach your phone (48h alarms
via your own calendar, no account needed).

Every item links to its source. Every fetch is snapshotted. Nothing simulated.

  → github.com/humoge7502/muhurat

18-second launch film rendered from the live product (Hyperframes composition
in brag-output/composition/ — edit index.html and re-render with
`npx hyperframes render`).
