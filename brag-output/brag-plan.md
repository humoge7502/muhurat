# Muhurat — brag plan

## Rubric answers

1. **What is the product?** Muhurat — a real-time opportunity radar for students: contests, hackathons and scholarships ingested live from public keyless sources, ranked by deadline, converted into device reminders.
2. **Who is it for?** Students (16–25) who miss deadlines not because they're lazy but because the opportunities are scattered across ten sites.
3. **The one-line claim?** "Never miss your moment — every deadline, on one clock."
4. **What must be shown?** The actual dashboard: MUHURAT wordmark, live ticker tape, countdown rings (82d/99d/106d arcs), type rails, the provenance footer ("nothing simulated").
5. **Tone?** `polished` with a `cinematic` hook — this is a trust product; the video should feel like a mission clock, not a meme.
6. **Format?** Landscape 1920×1080.
7. **Duration?** 18 seconds.
8. **Music?** Default bundled track, beat-synced cuts.
9. **CTA?** "muhurat — every opportunity, on time." (repo URL on end card)

## Creative laws check

- Hook is the miss-guilt line (2s).
- Scenes 3–5 show actual UI (screenshots/recorded capture from docs/assets).
- No SaaS language; copy is the app's own ("nothing simulated", "48h alarms").
- Scenes sum to 18s ∈ [15, 25].
